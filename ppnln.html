About This Project

An end-to-end automated data pipeline developed during my Accounting & Tax internship at PT Bank Central Asia Tbk (BCA) to streamline Foreign Value-Added Tax (PPNLN) processing and reporting. Designed to replace repetitive manual spreadsheet operations with a standardized one-click workflow, the system automates data extraction, tax validation, and report formatting—validated through historical dataset simulations with zero functional errors to significantly cut monthly processing time and eliminate manual discrepancies.

Key Features

  • Automated Data Ingestion & Cleansing
  • Built-In Validation & Error Checks
  • Standardized Report Generation
  • One-Click Execution & Backtesting
